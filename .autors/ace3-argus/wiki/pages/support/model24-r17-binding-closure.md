# Model24 r17 binding-closure boundary

The r17 package retains the r16 accepted source, exact four authenticated
overlays, compile closure, controller materialization contract, lifecycle, and
terminal contract. Its final `bindings.json` is generated by the materialized
`controller_model24_cascade.build_binding_document` from that source tree and
the exact bytes of its materialized `model24_tensor_map.json`; no predecessor
binding is used as the final artifact.

The r16 binding mismatch consists of six fields: the byte counts and SHA-256
values for binding source records 3, 4, and 7. The r17 binding-closure evidence
records those complete JSON-path differences and proves that the production
`validate_binding_document` rejects the preserved r16 binding while accepting
the generated r17 binding.

The production lifecycle imports and calls that same binding validator before
authority consumption, output creation, controller simulation, or payload
execution. Package validation binds the final binding, exact tensor-map hash,
binding-closure result, and the lawful consumed failed r16 ancestry. The r16
review, authority, consumed authority, and execution inputs are all
non-reusable.

The durable contract retains six positive fixtures and the 48 r16 negative
cases, then adds missing binding, substituted binding, actual stale r16
binding, and tensor-map mutation cases. Preparation is inert: canonical review,
authority, output, simulation, terminal, receipt, and log namespaces must all
remain absent.
