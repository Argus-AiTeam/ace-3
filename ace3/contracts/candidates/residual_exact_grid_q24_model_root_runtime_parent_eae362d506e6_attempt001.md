## State and numerical boundaries

For finite FP16 x, B(x) is its exact value times 2^24. Lift the authenticated
model-root embedding into signed int64 I and its negative-zero tag Z.
At each layer, compute exact T=I+B(actual S11 O), round T to the S12 FP16
view, feed that actual view to S13-S17, then compute exact
I_next=T+B(actual S17 D). S18 is RNE16(I_next), not a replacement of I_next
by the rounded view. The next layer consumes the authenticated paired
I_next/Z_next/H_next. No reference-derived correction or independently
editable carry is an operand.

Preserve canonical zero tags: nonzero I requires Z=0; exact cancellation
gives +0; only -0 plus -0 gives -0. Use checked signed 65-bit additions,
gradual subnormals and RNE ties-to-even; reject integer overflow, nonfinite
inputs, participating X/Z and FP16 overflow. Observable state requires
abs(I)<65520*2^24; no saturation. Serialize 896 ordered int64-little-endian
plus u8-tag records, exactly 8064 bytes, without padding or conversion.
Save/restore only completed-idle, independently admitted whole-layer state.
Reset/clear invalidate live and pending residual/KV validity; partial or
faulted output cannot be committed or forwarded.

Weights remain native asymmetric G128 packed INT4 in GEMM nibble order,
without qzero plus-one; scales, operator activations and K/V remain FP16.
Persistent residual/T are 64+1 bits. This is the explicitly labeled
`ace3-w4a16-operators-fp16-kv-q24-residual-proposal-v1` candidate,
**not unchanged strict W4A16** or promotion to W8A16/BF16/FP16.

S12's local operands explicitly become authenticated I/Z and actual O;

thresholds and the original-input independently propagated S18 binary64-v1
